"""
I run a small painting business. Below is a FICTIONAL customer enquiry. Turn it into a site-visit checklist I can use before I quote.
Rules: do NOT give any prices, hours or cost estimates. Use only facts in the message. Mark anything missing as "UNKNOWN – ask". Do not diagnose any damage. The stain's cause is UNKNOWN: never call it a leak or say it was fixed, repaired or dried out. Only ask when it appeared and whether it has changed, and list it to check in person.
Output:
1) Job summary (max 3 lines)
2) Table: Item | What the customer said | What I still need to know
3) Up to 8 short, friendly questions to text the customer
4) Things to check in person
Do not send, book or schedule anything.

Fictional enquiry: "Hi, we need the living room and hallway painted before my mum visits mid-December. There are some cracks near the window and a brown water stain on the hallway ceiling. We'd like something lighter than the current dark green. Can you do weekends? How much would it be? – Dana"
"""

Inputs above are FICTIONAL. Don't paste private customer or personal details.
