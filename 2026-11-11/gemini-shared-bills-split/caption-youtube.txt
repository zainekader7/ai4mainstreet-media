Shared house bills → who owes whom, with every step of the math shown.

The problem: some bills split by room, some equally, one housemate away half the month — and everyone paid something different.

Exact prompt (Gemini):
"""
Split our shared house bills for October. Everything below is FICTIONAL. Use only these numbers, round to cents, and show all your math.

Bills and how we agreed to split them:
- Rent $2,400 — by room: Priya 40%, Tom 30%, Lee 30%
- Electricity $162 — split equally
- Internet $75 — split equally
- Shared groceries $186 — Lee was away half the month, so Lee pays half a share (Priya 1 share, Tom 1 share, Lee 0.5 share)

Who already paid:
- Priya paid the whole rent
- Tom paid electricity and internet
- Lee paid the groceries

Output:
1) Table: Person | Rent | Electricity | Internet | Groceries | Total share (math)
2) Table: Person | Total share | Already paid | Owes (–) or is owed (+)
3) Who pays whom, using the fewest payments
4) A short, friendly message for our group chat with the amounts

Check that the totals add up to all the bills. Do not send any message or request any payment.
"""

Inputs above are FICTIONAL. Don't paste private customer or personal details.
