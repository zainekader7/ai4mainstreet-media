FAQ

Questions owners ask

Does the AI send messages to my customers by itself?

No. It drafts messages and reminds you. You (or someone on your team) read each one, then approve and send it from your own email, phone or Facebook page. The one exception is optional: Facebook's own free "instant reply" and FAQ features can answer DMs straight away with answers you've approved word for word.

Does it answer my phone?

No, and we won't pretend it does. This setup covers web forms, email and Facebook or Instagram messages. If you want call handling later, we can talk through options and their real costs.

Do I need to buy new software?

No. The standard setup uses Google Sheets or Excel, your existing email, Facebook's free business inbox and a free AI chat assistant. If a paid tool would really help, we'll tell you the cost up front and you decide.

Will this guarantee me more jobs?

No one can honestly promise that. What it does is make it much harder to forget a lead, a follow-up or an unpaid invoice. How much that's worth depends on your business.

How long does setup take?

Usually about a week from our first call. Your part is about an hour in total: the kickoff call, sharing access and the walkthrough.

What about my customers' information?

Your tracker lives in your own Google or Microsoft account, and you own it. We only access what you share with us for setup, and you can remove our access at any time.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Care plans are month to month with no contract. Everything we set up stays yours.
